# **TranzartX**

**Product:** TranzartX  
**Category:** African Art Commerce & Professional Networking  
**Primary Audience:** Emerging African Visual Artists  
**Product Focus:** Artist career development, professional identity, networking, opportunities, and art commerce

---

## **1\. Product Overview**

TranzartX is an African art commerce and professional networking platform designed primarily for emerging African visual artists.

The platform helps artists move beyond simply displaying their artwork online. It gives them a professional space to:

* Build a credible artist identity  
* Create and manage a portfolio  
* Showcase and sell artwork  
* Discover career opportunities  
* Connect with galleries, collectors, curators, and other artists  
* Build professional relationships  
* Receive personalized career guidance  
* Turn creative opportunities into sustainable income

TranzartX combines the functions of an **artist portfolio, professional network, art marketplace, and career discovery platform** into one experience.

The central product principle is:

> **TranzartX should help an artist understand where they want to go, discover relevant opportunities, and take meaningful steps toward building their career.**

---

# **2\. Problem Statement**

Emerging African artists often have difficulty turning creative ability into sustainable professional careers.

Their challenges can include:

* Limited professional exposure  
* Difficulty presenting themselves professionally  
* Fragmented opportunities  
* Difficulty discovering relevant galleries and collectors  
* Limited access to professional networks  
* Difficulty finding exhibitions, residencies, commissions, and open calls  
* Lack of structured career guidance  
* Difficulty turning artwork into consistent commercial opportunities  
* Dependence on social media platforms that are not specifically designed around an artist's professional career

Artists may have Instagram pages, portfolios, WhatsApp contacts, and scattered opportunities, but these pieces are disconnected.

TranzartX aims to bring these career activities into one environment.

# **5\. Product Principles**

TranzartX should follow five principles.

### **1\. Artist-first**

Every major product decision should answer:

> "How does this help the artist build their career?"

### **2\. Professional over popularity**

The platform should encourage professional growth rather than becoming another follower-count competition.

### **3\. Opportunity-driven**

The platform should help artists discover things they can actually act on.

### **4\. Personalized**

Different artists have different goals.

An artist looking for galleries should not receive the same experience as an artist primarily interested in selling artwork.

### **5\. Trust**

Professional relationships and commerce require credible information, transparent profiles, and trustworthy interactions.

---

# **6\. Core Product Experience**

The central TranzartX experience is:

**Career Goal → Personalized Recommendations → Action → Connection → Opportunity → Income → Career Progress**

An artist should be able to tell TranzartX:

> "I want to start selling my artwork internationally."

# **8\. AI-Assisted Artist Profile Creation**

TranzartX should allow an artist to describe themselves naturally.

Example:

> "I'm a young painter from Lagos. I mostly use acrylic and mixed media. My work explores identity and modern African life. I'm trying to get my first exhibition."

TranzartX can assist in transforming this information into professional profile content.

Potential AI-assisted outputs:

* Artist biography  
* Artist statement  
* Short profile description  
* Portfolio descriptions  
* Collection descriptions

The artist remains in control and should be able to edit generated content before publishing.
